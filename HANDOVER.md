# AUNSA website: guide for execs

Everything you need to keep the website up to date. No coding needed.

## Signing in

1. Go to the website and click **Exec login** at the bottom of any page, or go straight to `/admin.html`.
2. Click **Sign in with Google** and choose the Google account the president added for you.

You never need to say what your role is. The dashboard knows from your account, and only shows what you're allowed to edit.
Seeing "No access yet"? Ask the president to add that exact Google account.

Every change you save goes live on the website straight away.

## What each exec can do

- **Everyone:** edit your own Meet the team profile (name, pronouns, study, bio, photo) under **My profile**.
- **Execs with permissions:** edit the sections the president ticked for you. Suggested defaults:

| Role | Can edit |
|---|---|
| President | Everything, plus Exec access, Activity log, Handover & backup |
| Secretary | Club settings, Guides |
| Treasurer | Own profile only |
| Events & Community Officer | Events |
| Social Media & Outreach Officer | Events, Useful links, Instagram photos |
| Wellbeing & Equity Officer | Guides, Useful links |

The president can change any of these at any time.

## Common jobs

**Add an event:** **Events → Add event**. Fill in the details and sensory guide, upload a photo, then save. Once the date has passed, it moves to *Past events* and the home page carousel by itself.

**Publish a guide from Google Docs:** **Guides → Add guide**. Copy everything from your Google Doc and paste it into **Guide text**. Headings, lists, bold and links carry over. Use the buttons above the box to tidy anything up.

**Update the Linktree link or email:** **Club settings**. The Linktree button in the navigation bar updates everywhere.

**Membership form (Join AUNSA!):** make the form in Google Forms, then in the form's **Responses** tab click **Link to Sheets**, so every sign-up lands in a Google Sheet automatically. Click **Send**, choose the link tab, untick **Shorten URL**, and paste the full link into **Club settings → Membership form link**. The form then appears right on the website's Join page. Create the form with the club Google account so it's handed over with everything else.

**Discord:** paste an invite link that never expires into **Club settings → Discord invite link**. It powers the "Join our Discord" buttons on the home page. To also show who's online, turn on the server widget in Discord (Server Settings → Widget) and paste the Server ID into **Discord server ID**.

**Change the home page Instagram photos:** **Instagram photos**. Up to 5 photos, each with a link to its post.

## For the president

**Exec access**
- Add an exec with their Google email, name and role. Ticking a role fills in suggested permissions.
- Tick **Create a Meet the team profile** so they can fill in their own profile.
- Change permissions, change roles (this updates Meet the team too), make someone a co-president, or remove access instantly.
- There must always be at least one president, so you can't remove the last one by accident.

**Activity log:** the last 100 changes, showing who made each one and when. Nobody can edit or delete entries.

**Handover & backup**
- **Hand over the presidency** in one step.
- **Download a backup** of all content, including photos and the exec list.
- A handover checklist.

## End-of-year handover checklist

- [ ] New president: added in **Exec access**, then use **Hand over the presidency**
- [ ] New execs added with the right permissions; people who have left removed
- [ ] Meet the team updated
- [ ] Club Google account, email, Instagram and Linktree logins passed on securely, not in a group chat
- [ ] Backup downloaded and saved to the club's Google Drive
- [ ] Useful links checked
- [ ] If you own a domain: who pays for it, and when it renews

## If something goes wrong

- **Locked out, or the president left without handing over:** sign in with the **club Google account**. It always has full control.
- **Something was deleted by mistake:** check the **Activity log** to see what happened, then re-add it. Backups contain everything.
- **A photo won't upload:** try a JPG or PNG. Photos are shrunk automatically, but extremely large files can still fail.
